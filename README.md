## Favorite Series: Percy Jackson & the Olympians

**Percy Jackson & the Olympians** is an exciting fantasy series following Percy Jackson, a modern demigod son of Poseidon.

### Key Characters & Gods
* **Percy Jackson** - Son of Poseidon
* **Annabeth Chase** - Daughter of Athena
* **Grover Underwood** - Satyr protector
* **Poseidon** - God of the Sea
